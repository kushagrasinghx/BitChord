# DMCA / Copyright reports — BitChord

BitChord hosts no media files. It is a player: it plays the user's own
local files and content the user's own accounts and configured sources
can already access. If you believe this repository itself (code, text,
images, or releases) infringes your copyright, tell us and we will act.

## How to file a report

Email the repository maintainer with:

1. Your full name, organization (if any), and contact email.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed.
3. Identification of the allegedly infringing material in this repo
   (exact file paths and, where relevant, release tag + asset name).
4. A good-faith statement that the disputed use is not authorized by
   the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the
   notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of
   the owner of the exclusive right allegedly infringed.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

Valid notices are actioned by removing or disabling access to the
identified material. A counter-notice may be filed by the contributor
whose material was removed; valid counter-notices are forwarded and the
material may be restored as the law allows.

## Repeat infringers

Accounts that repeatedly contribute infringing material, decryption
keys, credential-harvesting code, or features whose purpose is to defeat
age, region, or entitlement restrictions are blocked from contributing,
and their contributions are removed.

## What this repo will not accept

- Pull requests adding stream-decryption keys, signature-solving
  workarounds, credential-harvesting, or restriction-bypass features.
  These are closed without review.
- Issues or PRs linking to, requesting, or distributing copyrighted
  media, premium unlocks, or cracked sources.

This file states process only and is not legal advice.
